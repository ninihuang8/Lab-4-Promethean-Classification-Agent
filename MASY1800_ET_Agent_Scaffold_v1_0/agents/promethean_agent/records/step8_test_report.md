## Genuine observed weaknesses and smallest proposed revisions
### W1 Primary course-section misattribution
Observed: the primary general_et_finding says, "Under Section 11.5, return of the old state would indicate reduction and Breakthrough at most, not automatically Breakthrough."
Verification: Chapter 11 Section 11.2, printed p.144, supplies the suppression/reduction diagnostic; Section 11.7, printed p.150, explicitly states the at-most-breakthrough rule. Section 11.5 discusses the framework's development and status. The conceptual rule is correct, but the cited section is wrong. This is a methodological attribution error, not an invented C1-C5 source or a failure of JSON structure.
Likely cause: the instructions identify several governing sections but do not explicitly bind each diagnostic to its source location. This is a hypothesis about the instruction gap, not access to the model's internal cause.
Smallest proposed instruction addition: "Attribute the barrier/suppression/reduction rules to Chapter 11 Section 11.2 and the operational restatement in Section 11.7; Section 11.5 establishes the framework's provenance and status, not those rules. Check each section locator before returning the output."
Status: proposed only. No correction was made to the preserved raw response, approved instructions, or prompt packets.

### W2 Contrast contains an unnecessary primary-specific aside
Exact contrast application_finding passage: "Distinguish model construction, operational scheduling and disruption recovery: the scheduling study assesses construction of constraint-programming models in computational experiments, not demonstrated operational scheduling or disruption recovery; none of those manufacturing functions is the present application [C5][H1]."
This accurately excludes manufacturing functions and preserves C5's scope; it does not import a fab, six-month horizon, or manufacturing risks into the company. However, it consumes the contrast application finding with a primary-specific distinction. The instruction's application_finding bullet unconditionally requires that distinction, which explains this relevance problem directly.
Smallest proposed change: qualify that bullet with "For semiconductor scheduling applications" and require other applications to explain their own evidence-transfer boundaries. Preserve the separate rule preventing C5 from establishing fab effectiveness. This is a minor relevance improvement, not a reason to call the contrast substantively contaminated.
Status: proposed only; not implemented.
